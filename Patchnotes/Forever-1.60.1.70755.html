Classes

  • The counters of class talent resets and Legacy talent resets now recharge every 7 days (was every hour) Client only

Druid

8
  • Claw now deals 140% weapon damage at every rank (was 110%) Client only
  • Shred now deals 200% weapon damage at every rank (was 155%) Client only
  • Ravage now deals 435% weapon damage at every rank (was 350%) Client only
  • Rake hits harder at every rank: rank 1 deals 27 and 20 a tick (was 23 and 16), rank 4 76 and 42 (was 61 and 34) Client only
  • Rip deals more a tick at every rank: rank 1 4 (was 3), rank 6 19 (was 15) Client only
  • Ferocious Bite's damage and the damage each extra point of Energy adds increase at every rank: rank 1 17 and 70 (was 14 and 60), rank 6 102 and 330 (was 82 and 270) Client only
  • Starfire's damage increases at every rank: rank 1 113 (was 81), rank 7 533 (was 381), and it gains more a level Client only
  • New passive Poised: at full Energy and out of combat, your next ability costs less Energy Client only

Hunter

6
  • Aimed Shot's bonus damage roughly doubles at every rank: rank 1 40 (was 20), rank 6 350 (was 166) Client only
  • Sniper Shot's bonus damage doubles at every rank: rank 1 320 (was 160), rank 3 590 (was 295) Client only
  • Arcane Shot's damage increases at every rank: rank 1 25 (was 20), rank 8 280 (was 217) Client only
  • Serpent Sting deals more a tick at every rank: rank 1 3 (was 2), rank 9 135 (was 111) Client only

  • Trueshot Aura's attack power increases at every rank: rank 1 20 (was 15), rank 3 50 (was 30), rank 5 100 (was 50) Client only

Paladin

2
  • Holy Shield rank 4 now costs 240 mana and has a 10-second cooldown, as its other ranks do (was no cost or cooldown) Client only
  • Two-Handed Weapon Specialization now increases Holy damage too (was Physical damage only) Client only

Rogue

21
  • The talent trees are reworked: 7 talents become new ones, 2 talents get places of their own, Improved Distract is removed and 21 talents move Client only
  • Death Mark replaces Thousand Cuts: a finishing move that marks the target and stores part of the damage your attacks deal to it, per combo point, then deals it when the mark ends, up to a cap (was Thousand Cuts) Client only
    • Also found: Garrote and Rupture are stored at twice the rate
    • Also found: 25 Energy, 20 yards, a 10-second cooldown, 4 ranks
  • New ability Shank: an off-hand strike for 50% weapon damage that cannot miss and gives 15 Energy, for 5 Energy, with a 10-second cooldown Client only
    • Also found: A second form of it gives 25 Energy
  • Boot Knife replaces Cutthroat: Shank gets a chance to add combo points (was Cutthroat) Client only
  • Flash of Steel takes Dual Wield Specialization's place: Shank gives more Energy and has a shorter cooldown (was Dual Wield Specialization) Client only
  • Dual Wield Specialization moves to a place of its own with 2 ranks (was 5) Client only
  • Venom now works through Shank: Shank raises your Poison damage and your chance to apply Poisons for a while (was always on) Client only
  • Quick Getaway replaces Quietus: one more target can be Sapped, and Sapping gives 35% movement speed for a while, 2 ranks (was Quietus, 5 ranks) Client only
  • Vigor replaces Murder: more maximum Energy, 2 ranks (was Murder) Client only
  • Blood Splatter replaces Vigor: Rupture lasts 4 seconds longer and Garrote 6 seconds (was Vigor) Client only
  • Composure takes Relentless Strikes' place: Poised lowers Energy costs further, 2 ranks (was Relentless Strikes) Client only
  • Relentless Strikes moves to a new place with 5 ranks (was 1) Client only
  • Coup de Grace, renamed from Improved Eviscerate, now also raises Death Mark's damage and storage rate by 20% (was Improved Eviscerate) Client only
    • Also found: The former Coup de Grace, more Eviscerate damage against targets with your Deadly Poison, is gone
  • Efficient Strikes is the new name of Improved Sinister Strike (was Improved Sinister Strike) Client only
  • Ghostly Strike now also lets your next Ambush or Garrote be used without Stealth Client only
  • Mutilate loses its first rank: the talent teaches the former rank 2, and its ranks are numbered 1 to 3 (was 4 ranks) Client only
  • Flawless Execution now lowers Eviscerate's cost by 15 Energy (was 10) Client only
  • Seal Fate now has 3 ranks (was 5), Lethality 2 (was 5), Camouflage 3 (was 5), and Opportunity 5 (was 2) Client only
  • Improved Distract is removed Client only
  • New passive Poised: at full Energy and out of combat, your next ability costs less Energy Client only
  • The top ranks of Poisons get more charges: Instant Poison VI 250 (was 175), Deadly Poison IV and Wound Poison IV 240 (was 160), Deadly Poison V 240 (was 180), Mind-numbing Poison III 225 (was 150) Client only

Shaman

12
  • Lightning Bolt's damage increases at every rank: rank 1 16 (was 15), rank 10 353 (was 196), and from rank 4 on it gains more a level Client only
  • Chain Lightning now has a 2.5-second cast at every rank, costs more mana and deals far more damage: rank 1 215 for 245 mana (was 88 for 145), rank 4 510 for 495 (was 123 for 315) (was a 2-second cast) Client only
  • Earth Shock's damage increases at every rank: rank 1 20 (was 18), rank 7 531 (was 301) Client only
  • Flame Shock's hit and its damage over time increase: rank 1 hits for 25 (was 20), rank 6 for 292 and 80 a tick (was 166 and 44) Client only
  • Frost Shock's damage increases at every rank: rank 1 98 (was 66), rank 4 506 (was 283) Client only
  • Stormstrike now strikes for 150% weapon damage, with a 6-second cooldown (was normal weapon damage, 8 seconds) Client only
  • Riptide no longer heals at once, only over time, for less mana and with a 12-second cooldown: rank 1 150 mana (was 245), rank 3 230 (was 385) (was an instant heal and a heal over time, 6 seconds) Client only
  • Totemic Projection is renamed Totemic Call and moves your active totems to you, with a 30-second cooldown (was to a place you choose, 1 minute) Client only
  • Call of Thunder replaces Lava Burst in the talent tree: 5% more critical strike chance for Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning (was Lava Burst) Client only
  • Elemental Mastery is renamed Lightning Mastery and no longer speaks of Lava Burst (was Elemental Mastery) Client only
  • Elemental Reach no longer shortens Totemic Projection's cooldown Client only
  • Improved Ghost Wolf and Improved Lightning Shield move in the talent tree Client only

  • Wrack's damage now grows by 2.5 a level (was the same at every level) Client only

Warrior

7
  • Slam's cooldown is now 15 seconds at every rank (was 18 seconds) Client only
  • Improved Slam now shortens Slam's cooldown by 5 seconds (was 3) Client only
  • Improved Whirlwind replaces Raging Blows: Whirlwind's cooldown is 2 seconds shorter and it deals 30% more damage to the closest enemy, 2 ranks (was Raging Blows: less Rage for Cleave and Whirlwind, 1 rank) Client only
  • Spearing Strike now deals 75% weapon damage, and 75% more against Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets (was 40%, and 80% more) Client only
  • New talent Reckless Swings: your next melee attacks generate extra Rage, with a 15-second cooldown Client only
  • Flurry now has 3 ranks (was 5), Improved Tactical Mastery 3 (was 5), and Bloodthrill 2 (was 5) Client only
  • Enrage and Gore Drinker move in the talent tree, and the arrows at Death Wish and Blood Craze change Client only

User Interface

9
  • Your Battle.net broadcast can no longer be set from the contacts menu, and the option Broadcast Updates is gone Client only
    • Also found: Friends' broadcasts still show in their tooltips
  • The amount a unit's hit or heal took (the event UNIT_COMBAT) is now hidden from addons Client only
  • Nameplate aura icons no longer show a tooltip Client only
  • Friends' game icons can show which of a game's projects they play Client only
  • The action bar's end caps hide when they do not fit on the screen Client only
  • The Spell Damage and Spell Healing tooltips no longer say that low spell ranks trigger class abilities less often Client only
  • Group Finder entries' names get less room (was 228 wide, now 200) Client only
  • New for addons: whether a spell is its highest rank (C_ActionBar.IsHighestSpellRank) Client only
  • 63 interface code files changed, no picture Client only

World

2
  • The ground, water, maps, flights and boats are unchanged Client only
  • 96 new light settings and 2 new kinds of weather, and some weather gets a new ambient sound Client only

Sane UI

5
  • The database took this build and its hotfixes: 14 new and 139 changed spells, 3 new and 42 changed talents, 7 class skill lines, 1 new and 6 changed items; 2 spells, 1 talent, 1 achievement and 1 option gone
  • Social's contacts menu offers your broadcast in Retail alone, as Forever's own took it out; a friend's game icon shows Forever's sub icon
  • Nameplates' combat text writes Forever's hidden amounts as they are, and a blow blocked, absorbed or resisted whole as its word
  • The settings' option labels and Sync's pages no longer list Broadcast Updates
  • Nothing in the world moved, so no walking data or painting of the map was made anew
