General

3
  • Everything here is compared with build 70755 and the hotfixes the game held on November 3, the day before launch; the servers ran builds 70790, 70862 and 70888 in between, so their changes are in here too Client only
    • Our note: Blizzard has published no patch notes for this build. Its hotfix log of November 5 to 13 is compared point by point.
  • Since launch the servers have sent the client 1,326 older items, 41 auras and the areas of four dungeons it lacked (The Drowned City, Blackmaw Hold, The Shaper's Terrace, Krol'dok Stronghold), with the Drowned City's encounter Min'loth the Serpent: they are known now, not new in the game, and stand only in the list of every change Client only
    • Also found: 11 item sets the client always held count now, their items being known: The Gladiator, Dal'Rend's Arms and Spider's Kiss among them
    • Also found: One item is new in the build itself: a second Berdun's Trusty Rifle
  • The item restoration service is turned off while a serious bug is looked into Blizzard only
