# SaneUI Social: changelog

What changed in each version of SaneUI Social, newest first: a version's part is its file's changelog on CurseForge.
`saneui makechangelog` drafts a part from the commits; a person writes it for players.

## 0.3.2 (2026-11-18)

- The setup's hot corner and keys are on one page: choose how the corner opens the full screen, and set its key and
  the chat window's right under it.

## 0.3.1 (2026-11-18)

- First public alpha of SaneUI Social: the game's chat as a messenger, every message in a bubble, yours on the
  right, everybody else's on the left with their class icon and name in their class colour.
- Quiet while you play: no background, clicks go through to the game, and messages fade after a few seconds; turn
  the mouse wheel over the chat and they are all back.
- Left-click the screen's edge where the chat is docked and it fills the screen: your conversations, friends list,
  guild window and the group finder side by side. Drag them to arrange them, and keep several layouts behind tabs.
- Every whisper, and the chat of every party or raid you join, is a conversation of its own, whispers and groups
  apart, kept over a logout.
- Chat views with the chats you choose, and any of them as a chat window of its own on the screen.
- Channels the same on every character: tick one once and you are in it on all of them.
- One search over messages, friends, groups and conversations; drag over messages and press Ctrl+C to copy them.
- A setup the first time (again with `/sane setup social`): the hot corner, a key for the full screen, your chat
  windows.
- Needs SaneUI Core, which the CurseForge app installs with it.
