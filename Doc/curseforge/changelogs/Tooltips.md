# SaneUI Tooltips: changelog

What changed in each version of SaneUI Tooltips, newest first: a version's part is its file's changelog on CurseForge.
`saneui makechangelog` drafts a part from the commits; a person writes it for players.

## 0.3.2 (2026-11-18)

- A short setup, the first time (again with `/sane setup tooltips` or *Set up step by step* on the options
  page): when tooltips show, at the mouse or in the corner, the 3D model, and how solid they are, with your own
  tooltip beside it showing each choice at once.
- Tooltips show at once, as the game's do: holding Shift for them is now a choice of *Show tooltips*, no longer
  how it starts.

## 0.3.1 (2026-11-16)

- First public alpha of SaneUI Tooltips: the game's tooltips redrawn as tidy dark panels, each in the colour of
  what it shows (an item's quality, a player's class, a spell's school).
- Items show the quests they start or are needed for and where you stand with each, the professions that use
  them, and over a herb or an ore how likely gathering it raises your skill.
- What a vendor pays and how an item binds. Auction house prices come with SaneUI Economy, later.
- A spell's numbers in a small table; players and creatures with health, power, target, buffs and a 3D model.
- Hold Alt for the IDs, which of your characters have an item, your character wearing a piece of gear, and what
  other addons write into a tooltip, in one section of its own.
- At first a tooltip shows while you hold Shift: *Show tooltips* on the options page sets it to *Always*.
- Needs SaneUI Core, which the CurseForge app installs with it.
