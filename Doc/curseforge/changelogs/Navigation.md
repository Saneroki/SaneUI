# SaneUI Navigation: changelog

What changed in each version of SaneUI Navigation, newest first: a version's part is its file's changelog on CurseForge.
`saneui makechangelog` drafts a part from the commits; a person writes it for players.

## 0.3.3 (2026-11-15)

- The pin menus no longer list the route and the minimap's dots: the golden line to your map pin and what the
  game's minimap shows are always there.
- Fewer pins at first: quest offers, low-level, later and done quests, quest areas, rares, treasures and possible
  herbs and ore start off; turn them on in Pins.
- The quest log on the screen stays in sight at first (Hide the quest log at rest is off).
- The minimap's satchel keeps Pins and Tracking apart: Tracking holds the found herbs, ore and fishing pools, rares,
  treasures, books and your tracking spells.
- Classic windows can bring back the game's own quest log: its key opens it, everything else stays ours.
- The Dungeons button stands left of the zone list; the quest log's scroll bar at the side it is docked to.
- At a flight master each flight point's place, zone and price stand on three lines.
- Smoother questing: a kill that counts toward a quest no longer rebuilds every pin of the map (five times less
  work on the full map), and the pins are laid out faster.

## 0.3.2 (2026-11-15)

- First public alpha of SaneUI Navigation: your minimap and world map in one window. Move the mouse into the top right
  corner and the map fills the screen.
- Pins in the game's own look for quests, flight points, rares, treasures, dungeon entrances and services, each
  kind on or off.
- Routes to your map pin on foot, by flight, boat, zeppelin and tram, orange or red where enemies far above your
  level roam.
- The quest log over the map, a search for NPCs, items, quests and places, and the maps of every dungeon and raid.
- Needs SaneUI Core, which the CurseForge app installs with it.
