# Change it only on purpose: every set made after the change looks different from the ones before.
style: An adventurer's journal: cream parchment with soft faint clouds and an extremely subtle grain, bound in thin dark brown leather with a visible grain and neat saddle stitching. Leather and metal only as the binding: thin outer borders, slim stitched leather title bars, small stitched leather patch buttons set into the border, small dark steel accents used sparingly (corner caps, rivets, a ring, a buckle). Everything on the paper is pencil and paper, flat on the page: loose, clean graphite pencil outlines, rules and dividers, paper strips, folded paper tabs; never leather on the paper. Plain calm paper, no stains, no sketches. High fantasy concept art quality, neutral to every faction. Flat front view, no perspective, even light from the top left. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark.
