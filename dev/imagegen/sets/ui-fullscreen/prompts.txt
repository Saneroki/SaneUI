# Full screen views (Saneroki, 2026-10-21): each a collection of the separate windows and panels the game and
# Sane UI's modules give, laid out as one screen. Full creative freedom inside a few rough rules (the style
# line). qwen-pe.txt, qwen.txt, flux.txt and zimage.txt include this.
before: Concept art of a full screen view of a high fantasy role-playing game interface, filling the whole 16:9 screen, made of several separate windows and panels joined into one composition. The screen holds:
style: Rough rules, everything else is your own creative choice (materials, shapes, silhouettes, ornament, colours, light, layout): compact, the panels small and dense with slim borders; all panels one family of materials and shapes, joined to their neighbours by shared rails, seams, hinges or docking pieces, so the whole screen reads as one coherent, connected design; the insides of the panels are plain, quietly textured free areas where the game's live text, lists and numbers sit; inspired by high fantasy concept art and cinematic renders (Dungeons and Dragons, Warcraft), not a copy of any game's interface and not low-poly; neutral to every faction, with no faction colours, crests or emblems.
social: a large chat history with channel tabs and an input line, a friends list with online dots, a guild roster with ranks, a group finder with listed groups, and two small whisper windows.
navigation: a large world map of a fantasy continent with quest markers, a quest log with collapsible quests and objectives, a quest tracker, a small round minimap, and a panel with the zone's information.
combat: three talent trees of round talent icons joined by lines, a spellbook grid of spell icons with names, the action bars being set up, and a small panel of key bindings.
character: a character sheet with the hero's model between the equipment slots, a dense column of stats, the bags as a grid of item slots, the bank, a list of currencies, and a small panel with the character's other heroes.
journal: an adventure guide with a list of dungeons, a boss page with abilities and its loot table, a list of reputations with progress bars, and a panel of achievements.
trade: an auction house with a search bar and a long list of offers, a profession window with recipes and reagents, a crafting queue, and a mailbox with letters.
group: a raid of twenty-five small unit frames in groups with health bars, a ready check, a loot roll panel, a boss timer timeline, and a small group chat.
settings: an options view with a column of categories, panels of switches, sliders and dropdowns, a preview of the chosen look, and a small panel of profiles.
