Restrained, calm and readable. Flat front view, no perspective, soft even light from the top left. Text shown only as thin pale grey placeholder lines, no letters, no numbers, no watermark.

navigation-moon: A full screen view filling the whole picture, a slim title bar along the top with a small dropdown on the left and a close button on the right; below it a large painted world map of a fantasy continent seen from above, with small round quest pins, and on the right a narrow quest log of collapsible rows with small progress bars.
combat-moon: A full screen view filling the whole picture, a slim title bar along the top with a small dropdown on the left and a close button on the right; below it, side by side, three talent trees of round icons joined by thin lines, each on a faintly painted backdrop, and on the right a spellbook grid of square spell icons with placeholder lines.
character-moon: A full screen view filling the whole picture, a slim title bar along the top with a small dropdown on the left and a close button on the right; below it, on the left, an armoured hero standing between two columns of square equipment slots with a stats panel beside them, and on the right a large grid of bag slots with item icons, split into rows of bags under thin dividers.
docked-moon: A game screenshot of a fantasy forest at dusk with small interface windows docked at the screen's edges: a round minimap at the top right, a chat window at the bottom left, an action bar of square spell icons at the bottom middle, small party frames at the top left, each with slim borders, quiet and unobtrusive over the world.
