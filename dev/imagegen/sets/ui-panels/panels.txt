doll-ring: a character equipment panel shaped like a round medallion: the hero in the centre, the equipment slots on a ring round it, the weapon slots just outside the ring at the bottom, a tiny item level beside each slot.
doll-wings: an asymmetric character equipment panel with two curved wings: the hero in a tall narrow arch, the armour slots stacked along the left wing curving outwards, the jewellery and weapon slots along the right wing.
doll-strip: a narrow vertical character equipment strip: the hero's portrait on top, the equipment slots below in a tight staggered zig-zag of two columns, each slot with a tiny item level.
doll-stats: a compact character panel: the hero's portrait with the equipment slots tightly round it, and joined to its right side a narrow strip of stats in rows.
book-fan: a spellbook panel with a dynamic silhouette: the spell icons in a curved fan, the spell school tabs as small notches along the curved edge, the chosen spell's name and cost in a slim strip below.
book-folio: a narrow spellbook folio with a curved spine: two columns of spell icons with their names, the school tabs as bookmark tails.
book-ring: a spellbook panel shaped like a rune circle: the spell icons round a circular frame, the chosen spell large in the middle with its name.
bags-categories: a bag panel: many small categories (consumables, trade goods, armour, weapons, quest items, junk), each a slim header line and a dense grid of small item icons with stack counts, almost no space between them.
bags-wide: one wide inventory panel: all bags combined into one dense grid of item icons in category blocks with tiny labels, a slim footer with money and free slots.
stats-rows: a narrow stats panel: rows of attributes with small icons and numbers, grouped under thin section headers, a tiny portrait at the top.
