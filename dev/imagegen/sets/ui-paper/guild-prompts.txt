# The guild window of guild.txt and guild-ref.txt (Saneroki, 2026-10-21): its views as the guild module plans them
# (Doc/ideas/Guild.md), with made-up example content.
guild-board: the guild window's front page: a header with the guild's emblem, its name and a small line "48 members, 12 online", and at its right end small leather patch buttons (settings, sync, close); a row of leather section tabs under it (Board, Members, Armory, Loot, Crafting, Raids, Officers). The page holds several panels side by side: a list of members looking for help with quests, each row with name, zone and quest level; a list of planned dungeon runs with their start times and open places for tank, healer and damage; three small news cards; the guild chat as subtle pencil-drawn speech bubbles with an input line; who is where, zones with small class crests of the members there; items offered to the guild with their icons; and a podium of the three members furthest in the race to the highest level, first, second and third on stepped blocks with small portraits, names and levels.
guild-members: the guild window's member list: a header with the guild's name and the message of the day, small leather patch buttons for invite and guild info; a search line; a long dense list of members, each row with a small class crest, name, level with a thin experience bar, rank, zone, a short note and what they are doing right now, grouped under "Online" and "Offline"; beside it a panel of the chosen member: portrait, class, level, rank with small up and down buttons, note, recent history, their other characters, and buttons to whisper and invite.
guild-armory: the guild window's armory: on the left a list of members with name, level and item level; on the right the chosen member's equipment: portrait, name, level, race and class, item level and points, the eight armour slots down each side with item names beside them, the three weapon slots below, an empty space in the middle for the character's model, and a small bar chart of their item level over time.
guild-race: the guild window's race to the highest level: a large podium of the first three, stepped blocks with portraits, names, levels and medals, filter tabs (everyone, my class, online), the first of each class in a small line, and below a ranked list of all members with level, a thin experience bar, time played and when they last levelled.
guild-crafting: the guild window's crafting page: leather bookmark tabs for the professions on the left, a list of the guild's crafters with their names, profession skill levels and specialities, a list of recipes with reagent icons, and requests from members waiting for a craft.
