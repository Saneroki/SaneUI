# The single windows of views.txt and views-ref.txt (Saneroki, 2026-10-21): the character doll, the stats, the
# spellbook and the chat; example content only.
doll: a character window titled "Character": the hero standing full size in the middle of the page, eight equipment slots with item icons down each side of him, and outside each slot a slim strip with the item's name, item level and two main stats in small writing; below him the main hand, off hand and ranged weapon slots and a smaller ammo slot, each with its stats underneath; leather bookmark tabs on the left for equipment, stats, skills, reputations and currencies.
stats: a character stats window titled "Character", its "Stats" bookmark tab chosen on the left among equipment, stats, skills, reputations and currencies: compact pencil-ruled rows of stat names and values grouped under small headers (attributes, melee, ranged, spell, defence, resistances), a small portrait of the hero with name, level and class at the top.
spellbook: a spellbook window titled "Spellbook": leather bookmark tabs on the left for the spell schools, each with a small coloured icon; two parchment pages of spell icons in neat rows with each spell's name and rank beside it, page numbers and small page-turn buttons at the bottom.
chat-history: a chat window titled "Chat" with channel tabs as small leather bookmark tabs on the left (general, guild, party, whispers, trade): the page holds a history of chat messages, each a subtle hand-drawn speech bubble with a loose pencil outline and no leather, the bubbles of others on the left and the player's own on the right; inside each bubble a tiny round class portrait, the character's name, the channel and a timestamp in small writing, then the message text; a slim pencil-ruled input line at the bottom.
chat-bubbles: three speech bubbles floating over a dark, softly blurred game world, each a subtle hand-drawn parchment bubble with a loose pencil outline, no leather and no frame: inside each a tiny round class portrait, the speaker's name, the channel and a timestamp in small writing, and a line or two of message text.
