# screens, panels and windows alike, bound in the stitched leather of a picked leather window (stitches, grain,
# metal corners, bookmark tabs on the left); faint quick pencil sketches behind the content. The other lists of
# this set include it.
style: The look: an open journal of aged cream parchment pages bound in dark brown leather. The leather has a rich visible grain and neat saddle stitching along every edge, small dark steel corner caps and a few rivets, now and then a slim buckled strap; it stays thin, a border only. Every panel and window has a header: a slim stitched brown leather strip across its top holding the window's title in small engraved serif capitals and, at its right end, small stitched leather patch buttons; the header counts as border. Tabs are leather bookmark tabs sticking out of the left edge, each with a small icon in muted colours (deep red, green, ochre, slate blue). Inside, the parchment is plain and calm for text, lists and icons, ruled with thin pencil lines; small details break the rectangles: folded paper tabs at the ends of rows, a curled corner, a tiny note in the margin. Background decorations are very faint, quick, light pencil sketches with few strokes, like a loose landscape study in a corner (distant mountains, a ruin, a few trees), drawn so lightly they never fight the text over them; no dense or detailed drawings. Compact and dense, nearly all of the space used by content. The quality of high fantasy concept art and cinematic renders (Dungeons and Dragons, Warcraft), not any game's own interface, not low-poly; neutral to every faction.
