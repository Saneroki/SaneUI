style: The look: an adventurer's journal, an open book of cream parchment with soft faint clouds and an extremely subtle grain, bound in thin dark brown leather with a visible grain and neat saddle stitching. Leather and metal are only the binding: the window's thin outer border, a slim stitched leather title bar across its top with a small metal fitting at each end and its title space left blank, and small stitched leather patch buttons set into the border at the top right and along the bottom; small dark steel accents used sparingly (corner caps, rivets, a ring). Everything on the paper is pencil and paper, flat on the page, never leather: loose, clean graphite pencil outlines for slots, rows, dividers and section frames, section headers as pencil writing with a pencil rule, folded paper tabs and paper strips; some paper strips hang out over the leather border. Side tabs are small leather bookmark tabs sticking out of the left edge with small icons in muted colours. Plain, calm paper for content, no stains, at most a very faint, light, quick pencil sketch. Compact and dense, nearly all of the space used by content. The quality of high fantasy concept art and cinematic renders (Dungeons and Dragons, Warcraft), not any game's own interface, not low-poly; neutral to every faction.
