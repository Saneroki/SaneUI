# The narrowed look (Saneroki, 2026-10-21): the field journal of leather, paper and cloth that Saneroki picked,
# made far quieter: the art only a thin background to the content; leather and metal for the bindings and belts,
# pencil sketch strokes for every border on the paper, leather patches for the buttons. The other lists include it.
style: The look: open books and loose sheets of paper. Leather and metal only on the outside: dark brown leather as a very thin binding, belt or strap along a window's edges, with small dark metal accents (corner caps, rivets, a tiny buckle) used sparingly. Everything on the paper is drawn by hand in pencil: the borders of the slots, the frames round the hero and the content, the dividers and the decorations are loose, artist-drawn pencil sketch strokes on worn parchment, flat on the page, never raised or carved 3D borders; faint pencil and ink sketches (maps, studies of armour and creatures, notes in the margins) as quiet background. Tabs are cloth ribbon bookmarks; buttons are small stitched leather patches; a title bar, where there is one, is a slim leather strip. No scroll rods, no banners, no crests or title plaques, nothing that takes room without holding content; the art reads as a quiet background so the content inside (icons, numbers, text) stands out. Compact and dense, nearly all of the space used by content. The quality of high fantasy concept art and cinematic renders (Dungeons and Dragons, Warcraft), not any game's own interface, not low-poly; neutral to every faction.
